## Purpose

For 30 days, Victor may rely on its existing Bedrock request planner to interpret natural Founder commands without requiring the Founder to repeat a narrow keyword such as `execute`, `run`, or `start`.

Natural imperatives such as `check karo`, `dekh lena`, `bhej dena`, `remind karva dena`, `schedule`, `search`, `read`, `note`, and similar wording may pass the V2 intent gate as execution commands during the window. The Bedrock planner remains responsible for semantic mode and target selection.

## Boundaries that do not change

This temporary authority does not grant or expand RED authority. Existing deterministic security, credential, destructive-action, authority-expansion, spend/payment, production-autonomy, STOP/PAUSE, Action Contract, capability lease, watchdog, and fail-closed controls remain authoritative.

Production autonomy remains OFF. Consequential execution remains Founder-command initiated.

## Expiry behavior

At the end of the 30-day window, temporary natural-imperative authority expires automatically. It must not silently continue. Extension requires a new explicit Founder approval and an auditable update of the authority expiry.

## Learning objective

The period is intended to let Victor operate with the existing Bedrock semantic planner under real Founder phrasing so that observed successful and corrected interactions can inform Victor's existing conversation/memory/experience mechanisms. This authorization does not permit the model to learn or grant itself new security or authority boundaries.
